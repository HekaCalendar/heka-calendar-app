13-Month Grid

Every month has 28 days. Every date falls on the same weekday within the year. The structure removes the friction of the Gregorian calendar.

Moon & Civil Overlay

See moon phases and Gregorian civil dates alongside HEKA dates. Switch overlays on or off to match how you think about time.

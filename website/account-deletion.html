Account Deletion — HEKA Calendar

We respect your right to control your data. You can delete your HEKA Calendar account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

Email Request (Required for Cloud Account)

To delete your Firebase cloud account and all synced data, email us:

hekacalendar@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your account. We will process deletion requests within 7 days and confirm once complete.

Delete Local Data from Your Device

In-App (Local Data Only)
  1. Open HEKA Calendar
  2. Go to Settings → Advanced
  3. Tap Clear All Data → Delete Everything

This permanently removes all notes, journal entries, settings, and profiles stored locally on your device. It does not delete your cloud account.

Export Your Data Before Deletion

  1. Open HEKA Calendar
  2. Go to Settings → Advanced → Data Export
  3. Tap Export JSON

A JSON file containing all your calendar data, journal entries, and settings will download to your device.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email-based)
  • All cloud-synced calendar data, journal entries, and tasks
  • Your birth chart profiles and celestial preferences
  • Cosmic Circle friend connections and messages

What Data Is Retained

  • Local device data — remains on your phone until you use Clear All Data or uninstall the app
  • Anonymous crash logs — stripped of personal identifiers, retained for up to 90 days for debugging

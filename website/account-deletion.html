Account Deletion — HEKA Calendar

We respect your right to control your data. You can delete your HEKA Calendar account and associated data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

In-App Deletion (Recommended)
  1. Open HEKA Calendar
  2. Go to Settings → Data Management
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion

Your account and all cloud-synced data will be permanently removed immediately.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • All cloud-synced calendar data, journal entries, and tasks
  • Your birth chart profiles and celestial preferences
  • Cosmic Circle friend connections and messages

What Data Is Retained

  • Local device data — remains on your phone until you uninstall the app or clear app storage
  • Anonymous analytics — crash logs and diagnostic data (stripped of personal identifiers) may be retained for up to 90 days for debugging purposes

Export Before Deletion

You can export your data as JSON before deleting your account:

  1. Go to Settings → Data Management → Export Data
  2. Save the JSON file to your device

Need Help?

If you cannot access the app to delete your account, contact us:

Email: hekacalendar@gmail.com

Please include your account email address. We will process deletion requests within 7 days.

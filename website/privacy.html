PRIVACY

Your Data Is Yours

HEKA is built on a local-first principle. Your notes, journal entries, and birth chart data never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to enable cloud sync.

I. THE LOCAL-FIRST OATH

Data Storage

By Default, Everything Is Local

Your notes, journal entries, birth charts, and app preferences are stored on your device using your browser's or operating system's native storage. We cannot access this data. We do not back it up. It cannot be recovered if you lose your device unless you've enabled cloud sync.

IV. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

External Providers

HEKA integrates Swiss Ephemeris for astronomical calculations. This library operates entirely on your device—no birth chart data, location, or personal information is transmitted to external servers for astrological computation.

If you opt into cloud synchronization, encrypted data is stored using Firebase (Google). We also use Firebase Crashlytics for anonymous crash reporting to improve app stability. You retain full ownership of your data. Encryption keys are generated and stored on your device—we cannot access or decrypt your information.

External Storage: Storage permissions are used solely for the Print-to-PDF feature. We do not access your photos, videos, or personal media.

V. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Age Requirements

HEKA is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our services, please contact us immediately for deletion.

VI. INTERNATIONAL DATA

Cross-Border Transfers

By default, all data remains on your device in your jurisdiction. If you enable cloud synchronization, encrypted data may be stored on servers located in your region. For any transfer outside the European Economic Area, we utilize EU Standard Contractual Clauses and maintain appropriate safeguards.

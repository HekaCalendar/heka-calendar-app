📜 Privacy Policy

HEKA Calendar
Last Updated: April 16, 2026

🔒 Key Principle

HEKA Calendar is designed to work primarily offline. Most of your data stays on your device and never leaves. You control what (if anything) is shared.

Information We Collect

1. Calendar Notes & Journal Entries

  • Daily notes and reflections you write
  • Journal entries with mood ratings
  • Category tags you assign
  • Storage: Local device only (unless you enable optional cloud sync)

2. Birth Chart Data (Optional)

  • Birth date, time, and location you enter
  • Name (optional, for profile identification)
  • Calculated astrological charts
  • Storage: Local device only (unless you enable optional cloud sync)

3. Cosmic Circle Data (Optional)

  • Your profile (display name, avatar/photo)
  • Friend connections you create
  • Task rituals you create or receive
  • Messages with friends
  • Storage: Encrypted cloud storage (required for social features)
  • Note: You choose who to connect with via invite codes

4. User Account (Optional)

  • Email address (if you sign in via Firebase)
  • Display name and profile photo (optional)
  • Used for: Cloud backup and Cosmic Circle features

5. Notifications & Alarms

  • The app may send you local notifications for task reminders, celestial events, and moon phase alerts
  • We use exact alarm permissions to schedule precise reminder times you set
  • All notifications are generated and managed on your device
  • You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time
  • Storage: Notification preferences are stored locally

6. External Storage & Print (PDF Export)

  • The app includes a Print feature that can save calendar views and notes as PDF documents
  • Storage permissions are used only to save or share these PDF files
  • We do not access your personal photos, videos, or media library
  • PDFs are generated locally on your device

7. Community Features (Optional)

  • Participate in anonymous community voting for holidays and app features
  • Votes are aggregated and not tied to your personal identity
  • No personal data is shared with other users through community features
  • Storage: Cloud (only the anonymous vote itself)

8. What We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Precise GPS location (only your selected region)
  • ❌ Device ID or fingerprinting
  • ❌ Contacts from your phone
  • ❌ Personal photos, camera access, or microphone (storage permission is only for saving PDFs from the Print feature)
  • ❌ Browsing history or IP addresses
  • ❌ Advertising identifiers

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Display your calendar and notes
  • Calculate personalized astrological readings
  • Enable Cosmic Circle social features (if you use them)
  • Sync your data across devices (if you enable cloud sync)

Data Storage & Security

📱 Local-First Architecture

All birth charts, notes, and journal entries are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. We cannot access this data.

Cosmic Circle Security

  • Friend connections require mutual acceptance via invite codes
  • Task sharing uses unique, time-limited codes
  • All messages and tasks are encrypted in transit and at rest
  • You can block or remove friends at any time

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • ✅ Use the app completely offline (no account needed)
  • ✅ Export your data anytime
  • ✅ Delete all data by clearing app storage
  • ✅ Opt out of cloud sync
  • ✅ Control who you connect with in Cosmic Circle
  • ✅ Delete your account and all associated data

Children's Privacy

HEKA Calendar is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
